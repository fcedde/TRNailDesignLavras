Services

Nail design specialists

Modern techniques of polish, extensions and care for impeccable nails. Book via WhatsApp.

Gel Polish

Dries instantly, does not extend the natural nail, lasts on average 2 to 3 weeks, keeps intense shine and does not peel easily. Ideal for those who want practicality and durability.

Strengthening

Thin layer of gel over the natural nail. Does not extend the natural nail but reinforces it, helps prevent breaks and peeling. Lasts on average 10 to 20 days. Ideal for weak and brittle nails.

Extensions

Increases nail length. Made with gel, acrylic, fiber or polygel. Allows varied shapes and sizes. Lasts on average 20 to 30 days and needs maintenance. Ideal for those who want long, customized nails for longer.

Gel Bath

Layer of builder gel over the natural nail. Can give light structure and small correction. Does not extend the nails. Lasts on average 20 to 30 days, needing maintenance. Ideal for weak nails.
